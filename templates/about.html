onemoretime

I'm Emma. I have a B.S. in Psychology and an M.S. in Physiology & Biophysics from the University of Louisville, and I spent a summer in an NCI-funded cancer research program studying circadian rhythm genetics — the kind of work that trains you to be precise, patient, and comfortable with data. After that, I started a D.O./M.S. program, and one semester in, I made the harder call: I left, because I could see myself repeating a pattern I needed to actually break instead of push through again.

Since then, I've been teaching myself to build software — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Swift, and now Python and SQL. This app is one of a few projects I've built along the way, and it's the one that means the most to me, because it's not really about job hunting. It's about proving to myself, one small log at a time, that I can build a different pattern than the one I was stuck in.

If you're building your own "one more time" right now — good. Me too.
