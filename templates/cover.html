a tracker for starting over

onemoretime

you don't have to get it right. you just have to try one more time.

I built the first version of this app when I was deep in a job search, just calling it "EmmaGetsHired." It was a simple tracker — log an application, get a coin, watch a garden grow. It worked, but it was narrow. It only cared about one thing: did you apply today.

Somewhere in the rebuilding, I realized the job search wasn't actually my whole problem. I'd left a doctorate program after one semester — not because I couldn't do the work, but because I recognized a pattern I'd been in before, and I didn't want to keep making the same mistake in a different building. That's a harder thing to admit out loud than "I got a rejection email." But it's the more honest one.

So I rebuilt this app to track the whole picture — not just applications, but getting up, moving, practicing speaking out loud, taking care of yourself. Because starting over isn't really about one big dramatic comeback. It's a lot of small, unglamorous "one more times" — one more application, one more day out of bed, one more attempt at the thing that scared you yesterday.

If you're here because you're starting over too — from a job loss, a program you left, a relationship, a version of yourself you don't want to be anymore — this is for you as much as it was for me. Hit next, and try one more time.
